

## Scroll-motion reference review

Early observations from the two user-provided references, saved before continuing the review:

- [Sonic Lamb India](https://soniclamb.com/en-in): the landing opens with oversized, staged hero copy and a large product visual against generous white space. On the first scroll, the page moves from that sparse hero into a dark trust-stat band and a feature carousel with category controls. The useful pattern is contrast between calm space and a dense, high-contrast feature scene; product counts and sales treatments are not appropriate to CodeDarpan.
- [CodeSangam 2026](https://codesangam.cc-club.in/): the opening uses a full-screen near-black technical field, a centered oversized wordmark and strong orange concentric rings. After one viewport scroll, the live capture showed a mostly dark next region and a registration-status notice; this transition may be loading/scroll-state dependent, so no hidden interaction is assumed. The useful pattern is an immediate, recognizable technical motif and a dramatic full-screen chapter transition, not copying its event branding or text.

The reference review remains in progress; these notes record visible behavior only and are not an endorsement or a claim about either site's underlying implementation.
